I started a research stay at the [Max Planck Institute for Meteorology](https://mpimet.mpg.de/en/home) in Hamburg, through the [Alexander von Humboldt Research Fellowship](https://www.humboldt-foundation.de/en/apply/sponsorship-programmes/humboldt-research-fellowship).
During this stay hosted by Dr. Cathy Hohenegger, we will explore if the behavior of self-organization found in idealized simulations can explain variability in cloudiness across the ITCZ.
